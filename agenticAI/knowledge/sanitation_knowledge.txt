SANITATION CO-GOVERNANCE PLATFORM

ABOUT THE PLATFORM

The Sanitation Co-Governance Platform is a citizen-focused digital platform for reporting, tracking, and collaboratively verifying sanitation-related complaints.

The platform supports communication between citizens and administrators and aims to improve transparency, accountability, and participation in sanitation management.

The platform is related to Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) 6, which focuses on clean water and sanitation.

PLATFORM USERS

There are two main user roles:

1. Citizen
2. Administrator

CITIZEN FEATURES

Citizens can:

- Register and log in to the platform.
- Submit sanitation-related complaints.
- Select the relevant ward.
- Select the complaint type.
- Provide a description of the problem.
- Upload a photo when reporting a complaint.
- Provide location information when available.
- Track the status of submitted complaints.
- View complaint history.
- Provide a satisfaction rating after resolution.
- Participate in community verification of complaints.

ADMINISTRATOR FEATURES

Administrators can:

- Access the administrator dashboard.
- View submitted complaints.
- Monitor complaint status.
- Update complaint status.
- Review sanitation complaints submitted by citizens.
- Manage complaints throughout their lifecycle.
- Monitor complaints that require escalation.

COMPLAINT STATUS

A complaint can have the following statuses:

Pending:
The complaint has been submitted but has not yet been actively processed.

In Progress:
The complaint is currently being investigated or addressed.

Resolved:
The reported issue has been addressed and marked as resolved.

Escalated:
The complaint has exceeded the defined service-level threshold and requires additional attention.

COMPLAINT REPORTING

When submitting a complaint, a citizen can provide:

- Ward
- Complaint type
- Description
- Photo
- Latitude
- Longitude

Each complaint receives a unique complaint ID.

Complaint records also contain the reporting date and the citizen who created the complaint.

COMMUNITY VERIFICATION

The platform supports community verification of resolved complaints.

Citizens can provide a verification score for a complaint.

If the verification score is below the configured verification threshold, the complaint can be reopened and moved back to In Progress.

This allows the community to provide feedback about whether a reported sanitation issue has actually been addressed.

CITIZEN SATISFACTION

Citizens can provide a satisfaction rating for a complaint.

The satisfaction rating helps capture citizen feedback after the complaint has been addressed.

AUTOMATIC ESCALATION

The platform includes automatic escalation logic.

Pending and In Progress complaints are checked against the configured SLA threshold.

If a complaint remains unresolved beyond the configured threshold, its status can automatically change to Escalated.

PURPOSE OF THE PLATFORM

The platform aims to:

- Improve citizen participation in sanitation governance.
- Make sanitation complaints easier to report.
- Improve transparency in complaint handling.
- Help administrators monitor sanitation issues.
- Enable citizens to track complaint progress.
- Support community-based verification.
- Improve accountability in sanitation service delivery.

SDG CONNECTION

The platform supports the broader objectives of SDG 6 by promoting digital participation and better management of sanitation-related issues.

The platform provides a mechanism through which citizens can report sanitation problems and participate in monitoring their resolution.

IMPORTANT CHATBOT RULE

The AI assistant should answer questions using the information available in the platform's knowledge base.

If the required information is not available in the knowledge base, the assistant should clearly state that it does not have enough information rather than inventing an answer.
